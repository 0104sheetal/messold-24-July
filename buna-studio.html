Buna Studio crafts high-end conceptual lifestyle goods and apparel. Their digital flagship needed to feel less like an eCommerce listing, and more like a permanent digital retrospective. We worked directly with their creative directors to discard standard catalog layout parameters entirely, moving instead toward a spacious structural layout that prioritizes high-resolution editorial format assets.

The Conflict

Identifying the barriers holding back a premier global luxury brand.

Fragile Mobile Scaling

01

High-end purchasing decisions happen on mobile viewports. The legacy framework compressed high-resolution visuals into generic grids, destroying detail visibility and reducing session time.

Narrative Fragmentation

02

The brand storytelling narrative stopped completely at the Product Detail Page (PDP), instantly dropping consumers into cold transactional tables rather than rich lifestyle discovery.

Core Web Vitals Decay

03

Unoptimized multi-app tracking scripts, unmanaged render-blocking asset handling, and structural layout shifts resulted in an aggregate mobile performance score of 32.

Cart Checkout Abandonment

04

A rigid, multi-step legacy checkout path generated severe conversion drop-offs during high-intent purchasing moments.
