07 / Questions

Answered Before You Ask.

You own the delivered cuts and the raw files outright. What varies is the creator's usage licence for paid media — twelve months is standard, and perpetual rights can be bought per creator at booking. Organic reposting on your own channels is unrestricted.
Every asset includes one revision round. If a cut still misses after revisions, we reshoot with a different creator at no cost — the brief was ours, so the miss is ours. Reshoots caused by a changed brief on your side get quoted separately.
Yes. We take over briefing, scheduling, contracts and revisions for creators already on your roster and blend them into the same slate as new ones. Their rates stay whatever you agreed — we manage the relationship rather than re-negotiate it.
We deliver edit-ready assets and read performance back from whichever ad account they run in. Media buying is a separate engagement — plenty of clients keep it in house and use the read-back to brief the next slate themselves.
Roughly two hours a month: a kickoff call, one pass of comments on the review folder, and a look at the read-back. Everything between those three points sits with the producer running your slate.
We open a new slate roughly every fortnight. From signed brief to first cut in your folder is fourteen days at median — sourcing runs in parallel with concept lock, so the calendar never waits on casting.
Cost moves with creator rates in your category, how long you need the usage rights, and whether you need regional language variants — so an average would mislead more than it helps. Tell us the product and the spend you're working with, and you'll have a real number in two working days.
