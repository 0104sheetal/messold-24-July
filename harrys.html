THE BRIEF — 00%

Harry's sells a category most men buy on autopilot. The product is not the hard part — the habit is. Our brief was not to make razors look premium, it was to interrupt a default purchase, prove value inside a single scroll, and then hold the customer long enough for subscription economics to work. Everything below is built around that one shift: from transaction to retention.

Audience Architecture

Three buyers, three entirely different reasons to switch.

Segments were built from purchase behaviour and objection type, not demographics. Each one received its own creative track, landing experience and offer.
